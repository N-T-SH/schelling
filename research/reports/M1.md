## Findings that need your decision

1. **Unit of prediction (relates to C04).** A Psych-101 row is a whole participant transcript, and choices are mostly key letters whose meaning is in free-text instructions. I used one record per `<<…>>` span, state = preceding transcript (last 2,000 chars), and options = the distinct choice strings seen in that experiment. This drops experiments with more than 20 distinct choices (12 of the sampled ones, likely more in the full data) and means "option 0" is a key letter, not a semantic option. Alternative: only keep experiments whose options are semantic. Which do you want for M4?
2. **Contamination check is noisy on Psych-101.** On `heldout_item` it flags 243 of 400 held-out items (3 exact, 240 near; 190 from Psych-101, 53 from Twin) because the trials are templated (same sentence, different stimulus values). I reported them and did not remove any. For Psych-101, `heldout_item` is probably better defined by trial template or dropped. I did not change the definition without approval.
3. **`heldout_study` is uneven** (val 250,102 vs test 11,730 records) because the held-out units are studies of very different size. Persons are balanced to the 10% target. Say if you want study splits balanced by record count.

## Deviations and choices to review
