## Deviations and choices to review

- `Answer` validates that probs are non-negative, sum to 1, and that Noul answers have `[p_no, p_yes]` with `p_yes == probs[1]`. SPEC states these semantics but not that the schema enforces them.
- `HistoryItem` is not defined in the SPEC; I defined it as `question`, `options`, `answer` (index), per "(question, options, answer) triples".
- `DecisionRequest.state` is typed `str | dict[str, object] | list[str]` (SPEC: bare `dict`) to satisfy mypy strict.
- ruff and the formatter exclude `docs/` and `research/`; ruff otherwise reformats the Python code blocks in `docs/SPEC.md`.
- Heavy training dependencies are declared in the `train` extra but not installed or exercised in M0; the pins are untested until M3.
- Not done: `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CITATION.cff`, `nightly.yml`, `release.yml` (not required for the M0 done-criterion; later milestones).
